Jurisdiction Overview

🇺🇸 United States

  • FDA: Drugs, biologics, devices
  • Common Rule: Federally-funded research
  • HIPAA: Protected health information

Often multiple regulations apply simultaneously

🌍 International

  • ICH-GCP: Clinical trials for regulatory submission
  • Declaration of Helsinki: Ethical foundation worldwide

Form basis for many national regulations

🇪🇺 European Union

  • GDPR: Personal data processing
  • Clinical Trials Regulation: EU-specific requirements

Applies to research involving EU residents

When Multiple Regulations Apply

Key Principle: When multiple regulations apply to the same research, you must comply with all applicable requirements. When requirements differ, follow the more stringent standard.

Example: FDA-Regulated, Federally-Funded Study

Must comply with both FDA regulations (21 CFR 50, 56) and Common Rule (45 CFR 46). Many institutions use single IRB review under both sets of regulations.

Example: US Study with EU Participants

Must comply with US regulations (FDA/Common Rule) plus GDPR for EU participants' personal data. Requires privacy impact assessment and meeting GDPR data subject rights.

Example: Multi-National Clinical Trial

Follow ICH-GCP as international standard, plus comply with each country's national regulations. Obtain ethics approval in each participating country.

Disclaimer: This comparison provides general educational information. Always consult official regulations and your institution's compliance offices for authoritative guidance on which regulations apply to your specific research.
