Research Regulations

Comprehensive overview of human subjects research regulations across all major governing bodies and jurisdictions.

🇺🇸 United States Federal Regulations

FDA 21 CFR Part 50

US

Protection of Human Subjects

Establishes standards for informed consent and safeguards the rights, safety, and welfare of subjects in clinical investigations regulated by the FDA.

Topics: Informed Consent, Vulnerable Populations, Emergency Research
View details

FDA 21 CFR Part 56

US

Institutional Review Boards

Establishes requirements for the organization, composition, function, and operation of IRBs that review FDA-regulated clinical investigations.

Topics: IRB Review, Composition, Documentation
View details

Common Rule - 45 CFR Part 46

US

Federal Policy for Protection of Human Subjects

Federal policy for the protection of human subjects, adopted by 20 federal departments and agencies. The 2018 revisions modernized and strengthened protections.

Topics: IRB Review, Informed Consent, Exempt Research, Vulnerable Populations
View details

HIPAA Privacy Rule

US

45 CFR Parts 160 and 164

National standards to protect individuals' medical records and other individually identifiable health information, with specific provisions for research uses.

Topics: Data Privacy, PHI Protection, Authorization, De-identification
View details

🌍 International Standards

ICH E6(R2) Good Clinical Practice

International

International Council for Harmonisation

International ethical and scientific quality standard for designing, conducting, recording, and reporting trials that involve human subjects. Ensures data integrity and participant protection.

Topics: Clinical Trials, Quality Management, Investigator Responsibilities, Monitoring
View details

Declaration of Helsinki

International

World Medical Association

Statement of ethical principles for medical research involving human subjects. Foundational document that has influenced research ethics globally since 1964.

Topics: Research Ethics, Informed Consent, Risk Assessment, Vulnerable Populations
View details

🇪🇺 European Union

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

EU

Regulation (EU) 2016/679

Comprehensive data protection regulation covering the processing of personal data of EU residents, including in research contexts. Establishes strong data subject rights and accountability requirements.

Topics: Data Privacy, Data Rights, Privacy by Design, Data Protection Impact Assessments
View details
