# Regulations & Compliance

This section provides reference documentation for key research regulations, standards, and compliance requirements.

## Overview

Research activities are governed by various regulations and standards designed to protect participants, ensure data integrity, and maintain ethical standards. This section serves as a reference guide to these requirements.

## Key Regulatory Areas

!!! info "Content Development"
    Regulatory reference materials are being developed. This section will include detailed information on:

### Human Subjects Protection

- Common Rule (45 CFR 46)
- FDA Regulations (21 CFR 50, 56)
- HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR 160, 164)
- International guidelines (Declaration of Helsinki, ICH-GCP)

### Data Protection & Privacy

- Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- State and institutional privacy requirements

### Research Integrity

- Federal Policy on Research Misconduct
- Conflict of Interest regulations (42 CFR 50, 45 CFR 94)
- Financial disclosure requirements

### Grant & Award Compliance

- Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200)
- Agency-specific requirements (NIH, NSF, etc.)
- Cost accounting standards

### Laboratory & Safety

- Biosafety regulations
- Chemical safety requirements
- Radiation safety standards

## Using This Reference

Each regulatory topic will include:

- **Summary**: Overview of the regulation's purpose and scope
- **Key Requirements**: Essential compliance elements
- **Applicability**: When and to whom the regulation applies
- **Implementation Guidance**: Practical steps for compliance
- **Resources**: Links to official sources and additional guidance

## Important Notes

!!! warning "Authoritative Sources"
    This guide provides educational summaries and interpretations. Always consult official regulatory text and your institution's compliance office for authoritative guidance.

!!! note "Updates"
    Regulations may change. Verify currency of information with official sources.
