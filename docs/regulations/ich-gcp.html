ICH E6(R2) Good Clinical Practice

International

International ethical and scientific quality standard for clinical trial design, conduct, recording, and reporting

Authority:International Council for Harmonisation (ICH)
Version:E6(R2) - November 2016
Official Source:ICH Website

Overview

ICH E6(R2) Good Clinical Practice is an international ethical and scientific quality standard for the design, conduct, performance, monitoring, auditing, recording, analyses, and reporting of clinical trials. It provides assurance that the rights, safety, and welfare of trial subjects are protected and that clinical trial data are credible.

The R2 addendum (2016) introduced a risk-based approach to clinical trial management and quality management, emphasizing critical data and processes.

Key Principles

1. Rights, Safety, and Well-Being

The rights, safety, and well-being of trial subjects are the most important considerations and should prevail over interests of science and society.

2. Informed Consent

Each individual should give freely informed consent prior to clinical trial participation.

3. Scientific Rationale

Clinical trials should be scientifically sound and described in a clear, detailed protocol.

4. Ethics Committee/IRB Review

Trials should be reviewed and approved by an independent ethics committee/IRB before initiation.

5. Qualified Personnel

Trials should be conducted by qualified and trained individuals.

6. Data Credibility

Data should be recorded, handled, and stored to allow accurate reporting, interpretation, and verification.

Investigator Responsibilities

  • Ensure trial is conducted according to protocol and GCP
  • Obtain informed consent
  • Ensure adequate resources and qualified personnel
  • Ensure proper documentation and record keeping
  • Report adverse events to sponsor and IRB/IEC
  • Maintain drug accountability
  • Comply with monitoring, auditing, and inspection requirements

Sponsor Responsibilities

  • Ensure trial design is scientifically sound
  • Select qualified investigators
  • Provide adequate resources and training
  • Implement quality management system
  • Maintain trial master file
  • Ensure monitoring, auditing, and inspection readiness
  • Report safety information
  • Maintain drug supply and accountability

Risk-Based Quality Management (E6 R2)

The 2016 addendum introduced risk-based approaches:

Quality Risk Management

  • Identify critical to quality factors
  • Determine critical data and processes
  • Implement risk-proportionate monitoring and oversight
  • Focus on preventing important and likely risks
