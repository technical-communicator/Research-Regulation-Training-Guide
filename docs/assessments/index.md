# Assessments

Knowledge checks and assessments to verify understanding of research regulations and compliance requirements.

## Overview

Assessments help learners:

- Verify comprehension of training materials
- Identify areas needing additional study
- Track learning progress
- Demonstrate competency in compliance topics

## Assessment Types

### Module Quizzes

Short quizzes accompanying each training module to check understanding of key concepts.

!!! info "Coming Soon"
    Module-specific quizzes will be added as training modules are developed.

### Comprehensive Assessments

Longer assessments covering multiple topics or entire subject areas.

### Scenario-Based Exercises

Case studies and real-world scenarios to apply regulatory knowledge in practical contexts.

## Assessment Format

Assessments may include:

- Multiple choice questions
- True/false statements
- Scenario analysis
- Short answer questions
- Case study evaluations

## Completion Guidelines

!!! note "For Training Programs"
    If you're using this guide as part of a formal training program, consult with your training coordinator or compliance office regarding:

    - Required passing scores
    - Retake policies
    - Documentation of completion
    - Continuing education credits

## Self-Assessment

For self-directed learners, assessments provide a way to gauge your understanding and identify topics for further study.

## Creating Assessments

When developing new assessments, ensure they:

- Align with learning objectives
- Cover key regulatory requirements
- Include scenario-based questions when applicable
- Provide feedback on correct and incorrect answers
- Reference source materials for further learning
