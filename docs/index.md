# Research Regulation Training Guide

Welcome to the Research Regulation Training Guide - your comprehensive resource for understanding and complying with research regulations.

## Purpose

This guide provides training materials, regulatory information, and practical resources for research professionals working in regulated environments. Whether you're new to research compliance or seeking to refresh your knowledge, this guide offers structured learning paths and reference materials.

## Who This Guide Is For

- Research coordinators and administrators
- Principal investigators and research staff
- Institutional review board (IRB) members
- Compliance officers and quality assurance personnel
- Anyone involved in conducting or overseeing regulated research
