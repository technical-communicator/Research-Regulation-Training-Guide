Key Regulatory Frameworks

🇺🇸 United States

  • FDA 21 CFR 50 & 56 - Protection of human subjects and IRB requirements for FDA-regulated research
  • Common Rule (45 CFR 46) - Federal policy for human subjects protection
  • HIPAA - Privacy and security of health information

🌍 International

  • ICH-GCP (E6) - Good Clinical Practice guidelines for clinical trials
  • Declaration of Helsinki - Ethical principles for medical research
  • WHO Guidelines - International research ethics standards
